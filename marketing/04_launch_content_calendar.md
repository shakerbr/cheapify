## 1. Phase B Mission

Phase A built an audience that believes the problem is real. Phase B converts that belief into **installs and first orders** — then into habit. Every content decision here serves one funnel:

```
Aware (Phase A audience) ──► Install / first visit ──► First order ──► Repeat + refer
```

All Phase A publishing rules carry forward unchanged: one 9:16 master → three native uploads; Kurdish audio + Kurdish burned subtitles; Kurdish/Arabic captions; no English; zero emojis; brand checklist applies. The only change is the **CTA**: "join the waitlist" is replaced everywhere by **"download the app"** (getcheapify.com hub → iOS/Android/Web).

## 3. Launch Week (Day 0 – Day 6)

Daily posting during launch week — the highest-intensity period of the calendar. Coordinate so founding merchants post their own "we're now on Cheapify" stories the same week (supplied with a ready-made asset pack).

| Day | Post | Format | CTA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 0** | **Launch video (60s):** founder on camera — "Cheapify is live in Duhok." What it is, who it's for, download link. Pinned post on all three platforms. | Video | Download |
| **Day 1** | **How it works (45s):** screen-record walkthrough — search → Single-SKU page → compare sellers → Best Value → COD checkout. | Screen record | Download |
| **Day 2** | **First real Best Value comparison:** live in-app footage showing 3 Duhok sellers on one product, actual prices, who wins and why. | Screen record | Download |
| **Day 3** | **Founding merchant spotlight #1:** one store, their products, their Cheapify link. Merchant reshares to their audience. | Video/card | Download + follow store |
| **Day 4** | **Delivery proof:** an order placed → dispatched → delivered. Real timestamps, real Duhok street, 3,000 IQD flat. | Video | Download |
| **Day 5** | **Q&A / objection handling:** top questions from comments — "Is it really free to browse?", "What if the item never arrives?", "Do I pay online?" (No — COD). | Card/talking head | Download |
| **Day 6** | **Week-1 recap:** early numbers — downloads, orders, stores live (only if flattering), thank-you note to Duhok. | Card | Download |

**Merchant coordination (launch-week force multiplier):** every live founding merchant gets a kit — their store card, a "Find us on Cheapify" story template, and their storefront link — with a request to post on Days 0–3. Their followers are our zero-CAC traffic engine (GTM Play 1).

Cadence returns to 4 posts/week. Theme: evidence over claims.

| Week | Content focus | Example posts |

**Series continuity:** Price Detective and Build in Public continue — now with in-app footage. Price Detective posts compare *Cheapify prices vs. the old way*: the evidence machine now advertises the solution.

## 5. Weeks 5–8 — Make It a Habit

Cadence: 3 posts/week (sustainable steady state). Theme: retention hooks and organic growth mechanics.

| Week | Content focus | Example posts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 5** | **New-arrival & deal rhythm.** Weekly "Best Value of the Week" feature — one standout deal from real listings. Becomes a recurring franchise. |
| **Week 6** | **Referral push.** Public launch of the four-direction referral program ([`../platform/referral_and_growth_incentives.md`](../platform/referral_and_growth_incentives.md)): refer a friend for free delivery, **nominate a shop** for three free deliveries, merchants earn ad boosts and rebates. |
| **Week 7** | **Category expansion teasers + community.** Poll: "Which category should we grow next — groceries, fashion, or car accessories?" Lets the audience steer visibly. |
| **Week 8** | **Month-2 report card.** Transparent stats post — orders delivered, on-time rate, sellers live, biggest saving found. Accountability as content (the Reliable trait, again). |

**After Week 8:** continue the weekly template — Price Detective, Best Value of the Week, merchant spotlight, community post — until the Phase 2 expansion (Erbil/Mosul) warrants its own regional plan.

## 8. Failure & Contingency Rules

- **If MVP slips during T-7 countdown:** stop the countdown honestly — one transparent post ("we're taking two more weeks to get this right") costs less than a broken launch. Reliability is the brand.
- **If a post draws merchant backlash** (a seller feels attacked by a comparison): respond once, politely, with the verification policy — never argue in comments. Offer them a merchant conversation in DM.
- **If delivery problems occur during launch week:** apply the spec's accountable-delivery tone (§2.2) publicly — own it, state the fix, no deflection. A handled failure is better marketing than silence.
