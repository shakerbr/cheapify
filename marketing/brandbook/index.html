01
Strategy & Positioning

Brand Foundations

A brand is not a logo, a font, or a color palette. It is a customer's gut feeling about a product, service, or organization. Everything in this book exists to shape one feeling: "I am in control, and I am never being overcharged."

Mission

Cheapify exists to eliminate arbitrary price variation, price opacity, and the exhausting "Interrogation Tax" across Iraq and the Kurdistan Region. We empower everyday consumers with radical price transparency and single-click checkout, while equipping local merchants with turnkey automation that protects their retail margins.

Vision

To become the definitive commerce operating system for Iraq and the Kurdistan Region — where every physical and social retailer is indexed, every consumer purchase is verified for best value, and shopping is entirely free of friction, negotiation anxiety, and hidden fees.

The "Onliness" Statement

Validated with Marty Neumeier's Zag framework — the single sentence that proves Cheapify's defensible difference.

"Cheapify is the ONLY multi-seller price comparison marketplace in Iraq and the Kurdistan Region that indexes local social media stores side-by-side on a single-SKU catalog, eliminates the exhausting "DM for price" Interrogation Tax, and guarantees total doorstep landed cost with single-click Cash-on-Delivery checkout."

Single-SKU catalog No "DM for price" Ptotal = Pitem + Dtariff 1-click COD

How We Speak

Voice, Tone & Editorial Standards

One consistent voice across English, Kurdish (Badînî), and Arabic — adapting its tone to the user's emotional state and context.

The Four Voice Principles

Street-Smart & Practical

We talk like a trusted, financially savvy friend who knows the best shops in the bazaar. No corporate buzzwords, tech jargon, or elitist phrasing.

Direct & Action-Oriented

We respect the user's time. Instructions are concise, buttons are clearly labeled, and product specs are immediately readable.

Empathetic & Accountable

When delays or cancellations occur, we never deflect blame or write robotic apologies. We speak plainly, take responsibility, and solve.

Culturally Grounded

We celebrate the vibrant retail culture of Kurdistan and federal Iraq, honoring local commerce traditions while modernizing the experience.

08
UI Component System & Design Tokens

Design Language: Clean Flat + Soft Depth

Flat surfaces maximize readability, restrained depth signals interactivity without decoration, and disciplined PMS accents keep the Best Value signal unmistakable — the visual embodiment of Radically Transparent and Operationally Precise.

8.1 Core Principles

01

Flat White Surfaces

Cards and sheets are #FFFFFF over #DDE5ED / #F4F6F8. Never gradients, glass, or textures behind content.

02

Soft Depth, Never Drama

One diffuse ink-tinted shadow plus a hairline border. Never colored, hard-offset, or stacked shadows.

03

Rounded & Friendly

12–16px cards, 10–12px buttons, 999px pills — echoing Comfortaa and the logo's organic strokes.

04

Accent Economy

Blue only for actions and active states. Green exclusively for Best Value, verified, and savings.

05

Breathing Room

Minimum 16px card padding and 12px+ gaps between comparison rows. Dense data never feels cramped.
