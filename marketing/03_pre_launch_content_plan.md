## 1. Phase A Mission & Strategic Rationale

We post before the product exists — as audience-building, never as a product launch.

Every two-sided marketplace dies from an empty room on day one. Phase A exists so that on launch day, Cheapify already has (a) an audience that understands the problem, (b) a waitlist of shoppers ready to try it, and (c) a pipeline of founding merchants mid-conversation. We are not "announcing an app"; we are **documenting a problem the whole city already feels** and letting people watch the solution being built.

### The Three Jobs of Phase A Content

| Job | Audience | Success Signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Make price variation undeniable** | Duhok shoppers | Comments, shares, "this happened to me" replies, waitlist signups |
| **2. Recruit the Founder's Circle** | Duhok social-media sellers | Merchant DMs, demo requests, onboarding commitments |
| **3. Build founder & brand familiarity** | Both sides | Follower growth, profile visits, name recognition at launch |

### What Phase A Content Must NOT Do

- **Never announce features on a hard date.** No "the app launches March 1st" until the MVP date is locked. Every product mention ends with "coming soon" + waitlist CTA. A missed public deadline directly damages the **Reliable** pillar of the brand triad.
- **Never promise what the MVP may not ship.** If a feature is uncertain (live driver tracking, e-wallets), it does not appear in content.
- **Never attack named shops.** Price-variation posts compare *prices*, not people. Blur or crop out store names, logos, and faces. We diagnose the market failure, we don't shame merchants — today's comparison target is tomorrow's Founder's Circle member.

## 2. Audience Priority

Phase A speaks to **two audiences from one account**, weighted ~60/40 toward shoppers:

1. **Shoppers (60%):** All three consumer segments from the GTM playbook — young professionals, household shoppers, and deal-hunting youth — in Duhok (center, Malta, KRO, Semel, Zakho corridor).
2. **Merchants (40%):** Duhok social-media sellers with 1.5k–50k followers on Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok — the exact Stage 1 cohort from [`01_two_sided_gtm_strategy.md`](01_two_sided_gtm_strategy.md) §2.1.

Rationale: shoppers grow the waitlist and create the viral mechanics; merchants are the scarcer, slower-acquired side — every founding seller recruited pre-launch is worth more than 100 followers.

## 3. The Unified Cross-Platform Publishing Model

**One concept → one master file → three uploads.** No platform-exclusive content in Phase A.

### Production Rule

| Step | Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Master format** | Vertical **9:16** video (TikTok/Reels/FB Reels native) or 1:1 square for static carousels |
| **Export** | One clean, watermark-free master file |
| **Upload** | Natively uploaded to each platform separately — never share a TikTok-watermarked file to IG/FB (downranked) |
| **Caption** | Same core message on all three; adapted per platform norms — FB tolerates 2–3 short paragraphs, IG medium, TikTok short + 3–5 hashtags |
| **Hashtags** | Kurdish/Arabic-local mix: `#دهوک` `#دھۆک` `#Duhok` `#Kurdistan` `#cheapify` (max 3–5 on TikTok, up to 10 on IG) |
| **Posting time** | 18:00–21:00 Duhok time (peak evening scroll); Fri 13:00–15:00 for after-prayer browsing |

### Language & Subtitle Policy (Locked)

| Layer | Rule |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Video audio** | **Badînî Kurdish** — Duhok is the launch market; Kurdish-first signals this is built *for* them, not translated at them |
| **Burned-in subtitles** | **Kurdish script only** — one track. ~70–80% of feed video is watched muted; the core audience gets the message with sound off. **Two stacked subtitle tracks are prohibited** — they consume ~25% of a 9:16 frame and viewers read neither |
| **Caption text** | **Kurdish first, Arabic second** in the same caption — one post serves both literacies, keeps engagement signal unified |
| **Separate language posts** | **Prohibited** — splits algorithm learning and triples feed noise |
| **English** | **Excluded** from Phase A content — adds nothing for Duhok shoppers/merchants and dilutes the "built for us" signal. Reserved for later investor/B2B material |
| **Later expansion** | When targeting Baghdad/Basra in Phase 3, the same footage is re-exported with **Arabic** burned subtitles |

## 4. The Five Content Series

Phase A runs on five named, repeatable series. Every post belongs to exactly one series — this keeps production systematic and lets us measure which pillar performs.

### Series 1 — **Price Detective** (shoppers) · flagship

Real, verifiable price-variation evidence from Duhok shops and social sellers.

- **Format:** Screenshot/photo comparison or 20–40s screen-record video. Same product, two sellers, two prices.
- **Rule:** Real prices only, with date and source noted ("screenshot taken 12 Oct"). Store names blurred. If the price gap isn't verified, it doesn't post.
- **Hook pattern:** *"Same charger. Same city. 6,000 IQD difference."*
- **CTA:** "Seen a bigger gap? Send it to us" → feeds the Report-a-Price-Variation bounty and the merchant pipeline.
- **CTA (soft):** "Cheapify is being built to end this. Join the waitlist — link in bio."

### Series 2 — **DM Hell** (shoppers) · viral/comedy

Relatable skits dramatizing the "Interrogation Tax": DM for price → "check DM" → ghosting → repeat.

- **Format:** 15–30s acted or screen-recorded skit, POV style. The GTM playbook's "DM Interrogation" concept (§3.3, Play 2) is the template — minus any in-app footage.
- **Tone:** Street-smart humor per the voice spec — we're witty about the *system*, never mocking a person.
- **CTA:** "Tag someone who waits 4 hours for a price reply." / Waitlist mention in caption only.

### Series 3 — **Build in Public** (both) · trust

The founder engineering journey — Shakir building the marketplace in-house.

- **Format:** Screen recordings of real UI progress, design decisions, code snippets, or talking-head clips explaining *why* a choice was made (e.g. "why one product = one page").
- **Tone:** Transparent and humble — including honest "this was harder than expected" moments. This is the **Reliable** trait made visible.
- **CTA:** "Follow the build" / waitlist.
- **Value:** Costs $0 (founder skill), differentiates us from anonymous page-commerce, and recruits merchants who trust the engineer.

### Series 4 — **Founder's Circle** (merchants) · recruitment

Direct value proposition to Duhok social sellers, aligned with the outreach scripts in the GTM playbook (§2.3).

- **Format:** Talking-head or motion-card videos addressing seller pain: *"How many hours did you spend in DMs today?"* → 0% Month-1 commission → free ad boosts → Verified Founding Merchant badge.
- **CTA:** "DM us 'FOUNDING' or tap the link — first 20 Duhok stores only." Scarcity is real (20 slots) — use it.
- **Cross-sell:** Every Price Detective post quietly doubles as merchant recruitment — a seller who sees shoppers demanding price transparency sees their future storefront.

### Series 5 — **Bazaar Wisdom** (shoppers) · community/interactive

Light, shareable local commerce content — polls, price-guessing games, "what does X cost in Duhok today?" quizzes.

- **Format:** Polls in stories, static quiz cards, comment-bait questions.
- **Examples:** "Guess the price difference — answer in comments", "Which neighborhood in Duhok is cheapest for phone accessories?", "Rate this week's worst 'DM for price' reply (anonymous)".
- **CTA:** Engagement itself; occasional waitlist reminder.

## 5. The Repeatable Weekly Template

**Cadence: 5 posts/week** — sustainable for a founder-led team indefinitely. The template cycles weekly; only the topics change. Posting window 18:00–21:00 Duhok time unless noted.

| Day | Series | Format | Primary Audience | CTA |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Saturday** | Price Detective | Screenshot comparison or 30s video | Shoppers | Send us your price gaps / waitlist |
| **Sunday** | DM Hell | 15–30s skit reel | Shoppers | Tag a friend / waitlist |
| **Monday** | Founder's Circle | Talking-head or motion card | Merchants | DM "FOUNDING" / link in bio |
| **Wednesday** | Build in Public | Screen record / talking head | Both | Follow the build / waitlist |
| **Thursday** | Bazaar Wisdom | Poll, quiz, or static card | Shoppers | Comment / share |

Tuesday and Friday are rest/buffer days — used for community replies, DM responses, and rescheduling if a post slips. Stories (behind-the-scenes, poll stickers, reshares of best posts) run ad-hoc on any day and don't count toward the 5.

## 6. The 4-Week Starter Cycle

The template repeats until launch. These four weeks seed each series with concrete topics; weeks 5+ rotate themes (electronics → beauty → home & kitchen → fashion → grocery) so no category dominates.

### Week 1 — "The Problem Exists"

| Day | Post |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sat | **Price Detective:** Phone charger, two Duhok sellers, ~5,000+ IQD gap. Static carousel: photo, price A, price B, gap math. |
| Sun | **DM Hell:** Skit — "How much?" → 4h silence → "check DM" → ghost. End card: «دەمێ بەرامبەرپێچاڤێ دویە» ("time for a change"). |
| Mon | **Founder's Circle intro:** Who we are + the 20-slot program. Motion card: 0% Month 1, free ads, founding badge. |
| Wed | **Build in Public:** Show the Single-SKU master-page concept on screen — "why one product gets ONE page with all sellers". |
| Thu | **Bazaar Wisdom:** Poll — "Have you ever paid more because you were too tired to compare? Yes / Always". |

### Week 2 — "The Cost of the Problem"

| Day | Post |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sat | **Price Detective:** Beauty or personal-care item variation. Video format this week — show the two listings on-screen. |
| Sun | **DM Hell:** "Send your location + number first" — the data you give away before knowing a price. |
| Mon | **Founder's Circle:** Seller-pain math — "50 DMs a day, 85% never buy. Here's what 4 of your daily hours are worth." |
| Wed | **Build in Public:** The Best Value concept explained visually — "cheapest price ≠ best deal; delivery speed and seller rating count too". |
| Thu | **Bazaar Wisdom:** Guess-the-gap quiz — photo of a product, comments guess the price spread, answer revealed in next story. |

### Week 3 — "Merchants Are Heroes, Not the Enemy"

| Day | Post |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sat | **Price Detective:** Home/kitchen item. Add caption nuance: "The seller isn't cheating — the system just hides prices." |
| Sun | **DM Hell:** Seller-side skit — a merchant drowning in 60 unanswered DMs at midnight. (Builds merchant empathy → recruitment.) |
| Mon | **Founder's Circle:** Demo-style video — mockup of a merchant's storefront link in bio, order arriving pre-formatted. |
| Wed | **Build in Public:** Why delivery is a flat 3,000 IQD in Duhok — the courier math explained. |
| Thu | **Bazaar Wisdom:** Community question — "What's the last thing you bought on Instagram? Did you check the price anywhere else first?" |

### Week 4 — "The Alternative Is Coming"

| Day | Post |
| :--- | :--- |
| Sat | **Price Detective:** Best gap of the month — recap format showing 3–4 submissions from followers. Community validation. |
| Sun | **DM Hell:** The contrast ending — "What if the price was just… on the post?" Waitlist CTA prominent. |
| Mon | **Founder's Circle:** Social proof — announce founding-seller count ("7 of 20 spots taken"). Only if real. |
| Wed | **Build in Public:** Checkout flow teaser — 25-second order concept, marked clearly as work-in-progress. |
| Thu | **Bazaar Wisdom:** Poll — "Would you buy online if the price, delivery fee, and seller rating were all visible upfront?" |

**Rotation for weeks 5–12:** repeat the cycle shifting product category per week and upgrading topics with material generated by the audience (submitted price gaps, poll results, merchant questions). If MVP slips past 8 weeks, deepen Build in Public — progress footage is the one resource that never runs out.

## 7. Production & Quality Checklist

Every post must pass this checklist before publishing — it enforces [`02_brand_identity_and_guidelines.md`](02_brand_identity_and_guidelines.md):

- [ ] **Zero emojis** anywhere — icons from Lucide/Phosphor only (Preference 26).
- [ ] **Colors:** PMS palette only — Blue `#298FC2`, Green `#228848`, Ink `#101820`, Neutral `#DDE5ED`, White; Flash `#FFA300`/Warn `#FF8200` for deal/urgency accents with white text.
- [ ] **Fonts:** Comfortaa (display/headlines), IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Kurdish/Arabic), Inter (Latin/prices). No other fonts.
- [ ] **Terminology:** "Best Value", "Doorstep Total", "Verified Local Partner", "Instant Checkout", "Price Variation" — never "cheapest", "vendor", "DM for price" (except as the villain being criticized).
- [ ] **Language rules:** Kurdish audio + Kurdish burned subs + Kurdish/Arabic caption. No English, no second subtitle track.
- [ ] **Real data only:** every price figure must be verifiable — no invented comparisons.
- [ ] **No hard launch dates** unless confirmed.
- [ ] **No named/blurred-out-identifiable shops** criticized by name.
- [ ] **Logo usage** per brandbook — outline mark, correct clear space, no stretched/recolored versions.
- [ ] **One master file, three native uploads** — no cross-platform watermarks.

## 8. Infrastructure Required Before Post 1

1. **Waitlist capture:** a simple `getcheapify.com` landing page — headline, one-line pitch, phone-number field (or WhatsApp opt-in link). This is the destination for every "link in bio" CTA. *Without it, content has no conversion endpoint.*
2. **Merchant intake:** a dedicated contact path — WhatsApp Business number or DM keyword ("FOUNDING") routed to Shakir.
3. **Profiles:** identical Cheapify profiles on all three platforms — outline logo avatar, Tier-2 bio hook in Kurdish + Arabic (`داشکاندنێن ب ئاقل. فرۆشکارێن پشتڕاست. ئێک ئەپ.` / `صفقات ذكية. متاجر موثوقة. تطبيق واحد.`), getcheapify.com link.
4. **Asset kit:** reusable templates per series (Price Detective card, Founder's Circle motion card) built once in the brand system, so weekly production is a fill-in task, not a design task.
5. **Submission inbox:** a simple way for followers to send price-variation screenshots (WhatsApp number or DM) — feeds Series 1 and the merchant pipeline simultaneously.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 10. Transition Trigger → Phase B

Phase A ends and [`04_launch_content_calendar.md`](04_launch_content_calendar.md) activates when **both** are true:

1. MVP launch date is confirmed with ≤ 3 weeks remaining, AND
2. ≥ 10 founding merchants are committed or onboarded.

At the trigger, the countdown sequence in the launch calendar begins (T-7). Phase A series don't die — Price Detective, Build in Public, and Bazaar Wisdom continue into Phase B with updated CTAs ("download the app" replaces "join the waitlist").
