## 1. Executive Summary: The Two Sides of Cheapify

Cheapify is a dual-sided marketplace. To succeed in the Iraqi retail environment, the platform must solve acute, daily frustrations for both the **deal-seeking consumer** and the **local retail merchant**:

## 2. Consumer Persona: Ahmad — "The Smart Deal-Seeker"

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ AHMAD · 23 YEARS OLD · DUHOK, KURDISTAN REGION                         │
│ Final-Year Business Student & Part-Time Office Assistant               │
│ Monthly Discretionary Budget: ~150,000 – 250,000 IQD ($100 – $165 USD) │
│ Mobile Devices: iPhone (iOS) · Active on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 Background & Profile

* **Demographics:** 23 years old, lives with his family in Duhok. Balances morning university classes with afternoon part-time administrative work.
* **Tech Adoption:** High. Uses his smartphone for study, social connection, and daily entertainment. Highly active on Instagram, TikTok, and Telegram student groups.
* **Financial Psychology:** Budget-conscious and value-driven. Because his income is self-earned and finite, every 5,000 IQD difference matters. He refuses to overpay for branded products.
* **Typical Purchases:** Phone chargers, braided fast-charging cables, power banks, Bluetooth earbuds, gaming mouse pads, laptop desk accessories, and personal grooming items.
* **Payment Preferences:** Prefers **Cash on Delivery (COD)** because it protects him against scam sellers. Increasingly adopts **FastPay** and **First Iraqi Bank (FIB)** for instant mobile payments when trust is established.

### 2.2 Core Frustrations & Pain Points

1. **Arbitrary Price Disparities:** Sees the exact same Anker 20W charger selling for **18,000 IQD** on one page and **28,000 IQD** on another. He feels retailers pull numbers out of thin air.
2. **The "DM for Price" Friction:** Hates seeing a clean post with no price tag, forcing him to comment *"Price?"* or send a private message, only to wait 3 hours for a response.
3. **Missing Specs & Variant Blindness:** Needs to know if a charging cable is Type-C to Lightning or Type-C to Type-C, or what colors are in stock, but captions say nothing.
4. **Buyer Paranoia:** Constant worry that sellers give different prices depending on whether the customer appears wealthy or asks politely.

### 2.3 Ahmad’s Daily Shopping Journey: Before vs. After Cheapify

| Stage | The Current Social Media Experience (Instagram DMs) | The Cheapify Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discovery** | Sees a phone mount on Instagram. No price is listed. | Searches or clicks the Single-SKU product page on Cheapify. |
| **Price Check** | Sends 4 DMs to 4 different pages asking *"How much?"*. | Instantly sees 3 verified Duhok sellers side by side with live IQD prices. |
| **Variant Check** | Asks: *"What colors are available?"* Waits 2 hours for reply: *"Only black."* | Selects the "Black" or "Silver" variant chip; out-of-stock colors are disabled. |
| **Decision** | Worries he is still overpaying. Purchases out of fatigue. | Sees the **"Best Value"** chip on TechZone Duhok (14,000 IQD). |
| **Checkout** | Types out his phone number, address, and directions in chat. | Clicks "Order Now", chooses COD or FIB, and finishes in 25 seconds. |
| **Outcome** | Stressful, wasted hours, lingering feeling of overpaying. | Feels **smart, relieved, and empowered** knowing he got the best local deal. |

## 3. Merchant Persona: Kak Kawa — "The Local Bazaar Retailer"

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ KAK KAWA · 34 YEARS OLD · DUHOK, KURDISTAN REGION                      │
│ Owner of "Kawa Tech" (Physical shop in Bazaar + Instagram Page)        │
│ Inventory Scope: ~300 Active Tech & Mobile Accessory SKUs              │
│ Staff: 1 in-store assistant + manages Instagram/WhatsApp sales himself │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 3.1 Background & Profile

* **Demographics:** 34 years old, married with two children. Runs a tech and accessories business in Duhok, operating primarily through an active Instagram page (~8,500 followers) and WhatsApp Business, supported by a physical storefront in the central bazaar.
* **Stage 1 Strategic Priority (Social Media Native Sellers):** Kak Kawa represents Cheapify's **exact Stage 1 target cohort**. Cheapify intentionally prioritizes **online social media merchants** (Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok sellers) for initial platform onboarding rather than offline physical retail stores. These sellers already have high-resolution product photography, video reels, digital price lists, and established delivery courier habits. Onboarding them into Cheapify requires zero asset creation overhead and offers them instant salvation from DM overload.

* **Tech Comfort:** Uses WhatsApp Business, Instagram app, and basic mobile spreadsheets. Does not know how to code, does not own an e-commerce website, and has no interest in maintaining complex software.
* **Margins & Economics:** Operates on slim retail markups (15% – 25% gross margins on accessories). Cannot afford 10%–15% platform commissions without raising prices and losing customers.

### 3.2 Core Frustrations & Pain Points

1. **Interrogation Fatigue:** Spends 3 to 5 hours every single day answering repetitive questions on WhatsApp/Instagram: *"How much?", "Where is your shop?", "Do you have blue?"*. 90% of DM inquiries never convert to an order.
2. **Lost Sales Due to Response Lag:** When walk-in customers enter his bazaar shop, he cannot reply to online DMs. By the time he responds 2 hours later, the customer already bought from another page.
3. **Manual Data & Stock Chaos:** Tracks inventory and sales orders in paper notebooks and WhatsApp message histories. Frequently promises an item to an online buyer only to realize his assistant sold the last unit in the store 30 minutes earlier.
4. **Competitor Blindness:** Has no quick way to know what neighboring bazaar stores or Erbil pages are charging for the same power banks or gaming headsets.
5. **Delivery Coordination Headaches:** Manages local couriers manually by texting delivery drivers customer phone numbers and locations, leading to wrong addresses and returned packages.

### 3.3 Kak Kawa’s Fulfillment Journey: Before vs. After Cheapify

| Stage | The Current Social Media Experience | The Cheapify Experience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Listing Items** | Takes photos, writes captions, posts to Instagram, but omits price to boost comments. | Matches barcode to Cheapify master SKU; enters his price (e.g. 15,000 IQD) and stock count. |
| **Customer Inquiries** | Answers 40 DMs asking *"Price?"* and *"Color?"* while attending walk-in bazaar shoppers. | **Zero repetitive DMs.** The catalog displays specs, colors, and prices automatically 24/7. |
| **Order Intake** | Reads messy chat messages, deciphers handwriting, copies phone numbers into WhatsApp. | Receives a clean, pre-qualified order alert on WhatsApp and his **Merchant Dashboard**. |
| **Inventory Sync** | Discovers an item is out of stock after promising it to a buyer; deals with angry customer. | 1-tap stock update on his dashboard disables out-of-stock items immediately. |
| **Courier Dispatch** | Calls local taxi/courier, negotiates delivery fee, explains customer landmark directions. | System generates a standardized waybill payload directly to 3PL courier (local partner e.g. Fast Delivery). |
| **Outcome** | Burnout, administrative chaos, lost orders, and inconsistent profits. | **Time saved, organized operations, pre-qualified sales, and fair margins.** |

## 4. Cross-Persona Alignment Matrix

How Cheapify harmonizes the opposing incentives of buyers and merchants:

| Dimension | Ahmad’s Need (Shopper) | Kak Kawa’s Need (Merchant) | Cheapify Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pricing** | Lowest possible transparent price without haggling. | Maintain healthy profit margin without destructive commission fees. | **[Progressive Marginal Tiering](../platform/fee_structure_and_monetization.md):** 5% / 3% / 1.5% take-rate preserves merchant profit margins so sellers can offer their lowest bazaar prices. |
| **Information** | Full specs, accurate measurements, exact available colors. | Stop wasting hours typing the same specs into chat threads. | **Single-SKU Master Page:** Specs created once; merchants simply attach their price & stock. |
| **Ordering** | 1-click checkout with Cash on Delivery or local e-wallet. | Confirmed, serious orders with accurate address and phone number. | **Automated Order Dispatch:** Replaces casual tire-kickers with prepaid/confirmed orders. |
| **Trust** | Assurance that the seller is legitimate and products are genuine. | Fair recognition for honest pricing, fast delivery, and good service. | **Verified Partner Badge & Rating Engine:** Good merchants win the "Best Value" spot. |
