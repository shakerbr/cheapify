## Issues Documented

- **Prices entirely absent** — not high, not low: *withheld*. Discovery, specs, and marketing all present; the one number that matters requires a conversation
- **The interrogation tax is visible in-frame** — a real commenter asking the price under the post; hundreds of others DM silently
- **Even advertisers hide prices** — a paid "DISCOUNT" ad with no number: the discount is relative to a price never disclosed
- **Manual ordering only** — call/WhatsApp/DM; every sale is a human conversation, merchant-side cost invisible to the buyer
- **Comment-based price discovery** — prices leak only through public replies when they leak at all

## Verdict

Corroborates the survey finding (46% of shoppers rank "DM just to ask price" a top-2 annoyance; merchants' #1 headache is answering price DMs). This is the commerce model Cheapify replaces: structured price on a master page, not a phone call.
