## Issues Documented

- **122% cross-channel spread** (90k → 200k) on the identical SKU — ~$73 overpayment risk
- **2× spread inside one marketplace** — duplicate unlinked listings of the same product coexist
- **Discount anchoring on social posts** — an Instagram post quotes "90,000 instead of 120,000" for the same model other sellers list at 95–135k
- **Prices as prose** — every Instagram price lives inside caption text, not a structured field
- **Delivery bundling inconsistency** — one seller includes delivery, others don't say; totals incomparable
- **Fake-discount anchoring** — 137,200 presented as a markdown "from 140,000"
- **Sparse review signal** — 5-star ratings backed by 1–4 reviews
- **Slow delivery normalized** — 48–72h on the marketplace
- **Competitor self-admission** — iBazzar's "Found Cheaper? Tell us!" is a manual patch for the exact problem Cheapify automates away

## Verdict

A shopper doing *diligent* research across Instagram + two marketplaces still sees a spread from 90,000 to 200,000 IQD for the same device. This is the evidence anchor for the entire venture: the market doesn't lack sellers — it lacks a unified view.
