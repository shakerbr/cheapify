## Slide 4 — Solution

### Say

*Time: ~38s*

That's why I'm building Cheapify — the bright side of choosing. [click] Cheapify puts every seller's real price for the same product on one page. [click] Today, one product is scattered across pages and apps. [click] On Cheapify, every seller sits side by side, showing the doorstep total — item plus delivery. [click] Our Best Value badge goes to the lowest doorstep total, fastest delivery and best rating — ranking can't be bought. You order in seconds, pay cash or card at the door, and the store is paid automatically 48 hours later.

### Key numbers

| Number | What it means | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Doorstep total | Item price + delivery shown upfront (example: 92,000 + 3,000 = 95,000 IQD) | Fast Delivery (Duhok) courier quote, Oct 2026 [6] |
| 3,000 IQD | Flat delivery fee inside Duhok city | Fast Delivery (Duhok) courier quote, Oct 2026 [6] |
| 3 ranking rules | 1. Lowest doorstep total · 2. Fastest delivery · 3. Best seller rating | Cheapify go-to-market plan [8] |
| 48 hours | Return window after delivery before automatic merchant payout | Cheapify financial model [7] |

### How it's calculated

- Doorstep total = item price + delivery fee (e.g., 92,000 + 3,000 = 95,000 IQD).

### If they ask

- **Q: How do you know two listings are the same product?** A: Barcode first. Without a barcode, we match name, specs and photos, and a reviewer approves before it goes live.
- **Q: Can a store pay to rank first?** A: No. Ranking is strictly lowest doorstep total, then delivery speed, then seller rating. It is never for sale.
- **Q: Who handles the cash?** A: Never us. The courier collects cash at the door and transfers it electronically to Cheapify; we pay merchants automatically 48 hours after delivery.

## Slide 5 — Value proposition

### Say

*Time: ~20s*

For shoppers: [click] every price side by side, an order in thirty seconds, and cash on delivery — or card. For merchants: [click] real orders instead of 'price?' messages, a free dashboard for stock and payouts, and a fair fee — about 3.8 percent on a typical order.

### Key numbers

| Number | What it means | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ≈ 3.8% | Effective Cheapify fee on a typical 25,000 IQD order (950 IQD) | Cheapify financial model [7] |
| 30 seconds | Checkout design target for a shopper's order | Cheapify go-to-market plan [8] |

### How it's calculated

- Fee on 25,000 IQD: 5% × 10,000 + 3% × 15,000 = 500 + 450 = 950 IQD.
- Effective rate: 950 ÷ 25,000 = 3.8%.

### If they ask

- **Q: Why would merchants with higher prices join if you expose them?** A: First, we target honest local stores that already price fairly (like the 100,000 IQD seller on Slide 2) and want volume without paying for ads. Second, a local Duhok store with a slightly higher item price still beats an out-of-town seller on Doorstep Total and same-day delivery (like Store A at 92k + 3k = 95k beating Store D at 91k + 5k = 96k on Slide 4). Third, no store is cheapest on every item — listing is free, they win on the items they price well, and they can adjust prices in one tap to capture ready orders instead of answering 'price?' DMs.
- **Q: Why would merchants pay a fee when Instagram is free?** A: Instagram costs them hours of 'price?' messages every day. We send ready orders with address and variant, coordinate the courier, and charge only on delivered orders — about 3.8% on a 25,000 IQD order.
- **Q: What does 'order in 30 seconds' mean?** A: Our checkout target: pick the best seller, confirm the address, choose cash or card.

### How it's calculated

- Launch area: Duhok 497,029 + Zakho 398,876 + Semel 166,319 = 1,062,224.
- Internet users (estimate): 1,062,224 × 83.8% ≈ 0.89M; governorate 1,599,871 × 83.8% ≈ 1.34M.
- Online shopping (estimate): launch area is 2.30% of Iraq's population, so 2.30% × $1,317M ≈ $30M; governorate 3.47% ≈ $46M.
- Regular shoppers at break-even: 585 orders/month ÷ 1.5 orders per regular shopper ≈ 390 shoppers (~$117,000/year ÷ $30M ≈ 0.4%).

### If they ask

- **Q: Which Duhok — the whole governorate?** A: We start with Duhok city, Semel and Zakho — 1.06 million people in the 2024 census. Akre, Amedi, Shekhan and Bardarash follow.
- **Q: Where do 890,000 and $30 million come from?** A: They're population-share estimates applying Iraq's 83.8% internet rate and $1.32B e-commerce market to the 1.06M census count. Duhok is 80% urban, so the real figures are likely higher.
- **Q: How do you get ~390 regular shoppers?** A: Break-even is 585 orders a month. At 1.5 orders per active shopper per month, that is about 390 regular shoppers out of ~890,000 online.

## Slide 8 — Business model

### Say

*Time: ~27s*

We earn only when an order is delivered. Our fee is 5 percent on the first 10,000 dinars, 3 percent up to 100,000, and 1.5 percent above — so on a typical 25,000-dinar order, our share is 950 fee plus 500 delivery margin: 1,450 dinars. [click] After 335 in variable costs — messages, logins, returns and re-deliveries — [click] we keep 1,115 dinars per delivered order.

### Key numbers

| Number | What it means | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5% · 3% · 1.5% | Marginal fee tiers: first 10,000 IQD · 10,001–100,000 · above 100,000 | Cheapify financial model [7] |
| 1,450 IQD | Cheapify gross take on a 25,000 IQD order: 950 fee + 500 delivery margin (shopper pays 28,000; merchant keeps 24,050; courier gets 2,500) | Cheapify financial model [7] |
| −335 IQD | Per-order variable costs: messages 90 + login codes 25 + returns 20 + re-delivery 200 | Cheapify financial model [7] |
| 1,115 IQD | Net contribution kept per delivered order (cash-on-delivery base case) | Cheapify financial model [7] |

### How it's calculated

- Fee on 25,000 IQD: 5% × 10,000 + 3% × 15,000 = 500 + 450 = 950 IQD (merchant keeps 24,050 IQD = 96.2%).
- Delivery margin: 3,000 charged to shopper − 2,500 Fast Delivery contract rate = 500 IQD.
- Gross share: 950 + 500 = 1,450 IQD (out of 28,000 doorstep total).
- Net kept per order: 1,450 − (90 + 25 + 20 + 200) = 1,115 IQD.

### If they ask

- **Q: What is the 335?** A: Per order: WhatsApp notifications 90 IQD, OTP logins 25 IQD, return handling share 20 IQD, and failed-delivery re-attempts 200 IQD.
- **Q: Is the 500 delivery margin real?** A: Fast Delivery quoted 3,000 IQD in Duhok city with a 500 contract discount, so 2,500 IQD. Signing that contract requires company registration — our first step after the pilot.
- **Q: And without the contract?** A: Profit is 615 IQD per order and break-even is ~1,060 orders a month — slower, still positive.
- **Q: What about FIB and Qi card fees — do they hurt profit?** A: No — SuperQi is 0% under Qi's 2026 merchant promo, FIB QR is 0.3% (~84 IQD), and the Central Bank caps card fees at 1%. Pre-paid orders don't fail at the door, saving the 200 IQD failed-delivery cost (see Appendix A2).

## Slide 11 — Financial feasibility

### Say

*Time: ~30s*

Our monthly costs are 435 dollars, so we break even at 585 orders a month — about 390 regular shoppers. [click] On our planned pace, the lowest point is minus 2,180 dollars in month three, we reach monthly break-even in month four, and all losses are recovered by month eight. [click] In the fast case: month three and month six. [click] Even at half speed, we're profitable every month from month seven.

### Key numbers

| Number | What it means | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $435 / month | Monthly operating costs after launch (652,000 IQD; $527 / 790,000 IQD from month 6) | Cheapify financial model [7] |
| 585 orders / month ≈ 390 shoppers | Monthly break-even volume (~20 orders/day at 1,115 IQD profit per order) | Cheapify financial model [7] |
| −$2,180 · Month 4 · Month 8 | Planned case: lowest cash point (M3), monthly break-even (M4), all losses recovered / payback (M8) | Cheapify financial model [7] |
| Month 3 · Month 6 | Fast case: monthly break-even (M3) and full payback (M6); lowest point −$1,800 (M2) | Cheapify financial model [7] |
| Month 7 | Slow case (half pace): profitable every month from month 7; lowest point −$2,750 (M6) | Cheapify financial model [7] |

### How it's calculated

- Monthly break-even: 652,000 IQD ÷ 1,115 IQD/order ≈ 585 orders/month (÷ 1.5 orders/shopper ≈ 390 regular shoppers).
- Monthly costs in USD: 652,000 ÷ 1,500 ≈ $435/month; from M6: 790,000 ÷ 1,500 ≈ $527/month.
- Low point composition (−3.27M IQD ≈ −$2,180): startup 1.70M + 2 build months 0.42M + M1–M3 operating losses 1.15M IQD.

### If they ask

- **Q: Isn't break-even when you pay the grant back?** A: Those are two distinct milestones, both shown on the slide: monthly break-even is month 4 (revenue covers the $435 monthly cost), and month 8 is payback — when cumulative profit has earned back every dollar of the $2,180 initial hole.
- **Q: Does 'all losses recovered in month 8' mean the entire $5,480 grant?** A: Month 8 recovers the $2,180 startup and operating hole on the planned curve. The rest of the $5,480 grant is either undrawn slow-case runway buffer ($575 not spent on the planned pace) or the $2,200 growth push that drives the fast curve — and even if you count the $2,200 growth push against the planned curve, cumulative profits earn all of it back by month 10 (or month 7 on the fast curve).
- **Q: Did you count the 0% first-month commission for founding stores?** A: Yes — as a dedicated $130 line in the grant (~200 orders × 950 IQD).
- **Q: What if the courier takes days to send you the cash?** A: Merchants are paid after the 48-hour return window closes AND the courier's electronic transfer arrives (contract target: 24 hours), so we never pay out cash we haven't received.
- **Q: What if orders grow even slower — would you have to pay $435/month out of pocket?** A: Never. Of the $435/month (652,000 IQD) modeled burn, ~$380/month (569,500 IQD) is discretionary growth and support spend (150k test ads, 225k order-gated incentives pool, 75k accounting retainer, 120k paid AI/SaaS tiers). The baseline infrastructure and messaging lines (Supabase Pro 37.5k + WhatsApp base 30k + SMS OTP 15k) total just 82,500 IQD (~$55/month, or under $15/month on free tiers), so we can dial burn down immediately if orders ever lag.
- **Q: What does the slow case look like?** A: At half the planned order volume, the lowest point is −$2,750 in month 6, we are profitable every month from month 7, and cumulative recovery takes ~20 months.
- **Q: Which exchange rate do you use?** A: 1,500 IQD per USD — the Central Bank of Iraq official rate set on 7 Oct 2026.

### How it's calculated

- Sum of the 5 blocks (6 computed lines): Pilot $100 + Registration $1,000 + Launch 20 stores ($710 kits/labels/app stores + $130 free M1 = $840) + Slow-case runway $1,340 + Growth push $2,200 = $5,480.
- Runway ($1,340): sum of slow-case monthly losses M1–M6 (596k + 524k + 429k + 318k + 95k + 37k = 1,998.4k IQD ≈ $1,340). Planned case uses only ~$765 (~$770 rounded).
- Monthly release caps (M0–M6): M0 $1,100 · M1 $1,180 · M2 $414 · M3 $836 · M4 $762 · M5 $613 · M6 $575 = $5,480.

### If they ask

- **Q: Why $5,480?** A: Every dollar is a real expense computed from our cash table: pilot $100, registration $1,000, store kits and app stores $710, founding-store 0% month $130, slow-case runway $1,340, and 4-month growth push $2,200. Zero idle cash, zero reserve, zero salary (see Appendix A9).
- **Q: What about the 1,000,000 IQD ($670) bank deposit required during KRI registration?** A: The bank only freezes 1M IQD temporarily during the 1–2 week registration process and unfreezes it once the certificate is issued. Our M1 launch and runway funds ($2,180) satisfy that bank certificate during registration and then pay for the store kits and runway — so we never ask GIZ for idle cash.
- **Q: What happens to unused runway?** A: Runway is released only against actual monthly losses up to the $1,340 slow-case cap. On the planned pace we use ~$765; the unused ~$575 stays undrawn and is reported back.
- **Q: What if you receive a smaller award?** A: $3,280 covers everything except the $2,200 growth push — including the full $1,340 slow-case runway so we survive even half-speed adoption with $0 founder cash. $2,710 is our minimum self-funding launch: it covers pilot, registration, 20 stores, and the $770 planned-case runway to month-4 break-even with $0 out-of-pocket losses (and if orders start slow, pausing optional ad/retainer lines inside burn keeps total losses inside the $770 cap).
- **Q: Why run the pilot before registration?** A: Because 15–20 hand-run orders cost $100 and verify real order size and delivery flow before we commit $1,000 to registration.
- **Q: How do you report progress after funding?** A: Monthly reports on five metrics: delivered orders, gross sales, active stores, repeat-order rate, and average delivery time.
