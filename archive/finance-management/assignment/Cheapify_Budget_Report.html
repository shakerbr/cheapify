1. Executive Strategy: In-House Software Engineering & Sustainable Profitability

In Iraq, over 80% of digital retail happens across fragmented social pages plagued by the "Interrogation Tax" (hidden prices, unlisted variants, missing dimensions). Cheapify organizes local retail into a structured Single-SKU catalog with side-by-side merchant price transparency and automated WhatsApp routing.

Why this financial model is compelling for GIZ approval: Because the founder is a computer engineer who develops the software, database, and APIs directly in-house, and produces all video marketing content, cash software development and creator fees are $0—saving over $6,000 in agency costs. With steady order volume and lean operational overhead ($156/mo), the business model demonstrates a healthy 88.6% gross margin and +37.1% positive net operating profit (+1,950,000 IQD) during the pilot, scaling to +$1,400 to +$3,000 USD/month in net cash profit post-pilot. The requested seed grant of $2,200 USD (3,300,000 IQD) strictly funds verified third-party operational bills: high-concurrency servers, Cloudflare edge security, WhatsApp APIs, local Meta ads in Duhok, and official commercial trade registration.
