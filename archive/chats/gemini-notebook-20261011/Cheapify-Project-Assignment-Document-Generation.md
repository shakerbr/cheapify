### Project Overview: Cheapify

**1. Problem and Solution**
* **The Problem:** 
  * In Iraq, online shoppers deal with huge, arbitrary price differences for the exact same item across Instagram pages and local shops.
  * Buyers lack confidence due to poor discovery, duplicate listings, and inconsistent seller credibility.
  * Local shop owners struggle to transition online without expensive setups or complex tech.
* **The Solution:** 
  * Build an organized multi-seller marketplace where multiple local vendors list their products under a single, unified catalog.
  * Give buyers clear, transparent comparisons to find the best deal immediately.
  * Provide verified local merchants with an easy-to-use digital storefront connected to familiar payment and delivery options.

---

**2. Innovation in the Project**
* **Algorithmic Deal Discovery:** Unlike standard social media browsing, the system flags duplicate listings and ranks identical items by fair pricing, stock status, and store rating.
* **Proactive Merchant Alerts:** If a seller is being undercut or priced out, the platform notifies them so they can adjust prices dynamically and stay competitive.
* **Local Payment & Delivery Handshake:** Deep, localized integration with everyday Iraqi payment rails (Qi Card, FastPay, and Cash on Delivery) paired with automated dispatch to local courier APIs.

---

**3. Founder Skill: Initiative**
* **Selected Skill:** Initiative.
* **How It Drives the Project:**
  * **Self-directed build:** Rather than waiting on funding to hire an agency, I use my software engineering background to build, host, and test the core MVP myself.
  * **Active field validation:** Instead of assuming merchant needs, I actively visit local shops in commercial hubs to pitch the concept, map their day-to-day problems, and onboard them directly.
  * **Resourcefulness:** Turning technical engineering skills straight into practical tools without waiting for perfect conditions.

---

**4. Customer Surveys & Feedback Plan**

* **Pre-Launch (Validation & Customer Discovery):**
  * **Target Audience:** 
    * End-users: Active online buyers on Facebook/Instagram in Duhok and Erbil.
    * Merchants: Small-to-medium retail owners selling electronics, mobile accessories, and clothing.
  * **Key Questions for Buyers:** 
    * "How often do you find price differences for the same product online?"
    * "What makes you hesitate the most before ordering from a local page (trust, price, returns)?"
  * **Key Questions for Merchants:** 
    * "How do you currently track competitor pricing?"
    * "What prevents you from listing products on existing e-commerce apps?"
  * **Format:** Short 5-question Google Form shared across local student/community channels, combined with quick 3-minute face-to-face interviews with shop owners.

* **Post-Launch (Operational Feedback & Retention):**
  * **For Buyers:** 
    * Automated 1-click rating via WhatsApp/SMS right after delivery: "Did the item match the price and quality shown?"
    * In-app report button for incorrect pricing or out-of-stock items.
  * **For Merchants:** 
    * Monthly check-in survey: "Are sales increasing?", "Is payment payout and delivery pickup smooth?"
    * Direct merchant advisory channel to gather feature requests for the seller dashboard.

---

**5. Value Provided**
* **To Shoppers:** Saves real money and search time by guaranteeing transparent, competitive prices from verified local sellers with trusted delivery.
* **To Merchants:** Lowers the barrier to digital commerce, driving new customer orders without charging high commissions.
* **To the Local Economy:** Formalizes local digital retail, keeping spending inside local businesses while promoting digital payment adoption.
