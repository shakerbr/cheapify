# gemini response

For a business process, you can break **Cheapify** down into its two primary operational workflows: **Merchant Onboarding & Listing** (getting verified products into the catalog) and **Order & Delivery Fulfillment** (from customer checkout to delivery). 

Here are the step-by-step business processes formatted as **Input $\rightarrow$ Steps $\rightarrow$ Output $\rightarrow$ Quality Check**:

---

### Process 1: Merchant & Product Onboarding

*   **Input:** 
    *   Merchant application / registration details (local physical store or Instagram/Facebook page).
    *   Store inventory data: product names, barcodes/SKUs, specs, photos, stock count, and price in IQD.
*   **Steps:**
    1.  **Verification:** Review seller identity, store location, and phone number.
    2.  **Catalog Matching:** Check product barcodes or serials against the central database to identify existing items.
    3.  **Listing Assignment:** If the item already exists, attach the merchant’s stock and price to the existing shared product page; if new, generate a clean catalog entry.
    4.  **Price & Alert Engine Setup:** Activate system alerts to track price competitiveness against other local sellers.
    5.  **Storefront Activation:** Publish the merchant’s profile and list products live on the platform.
*   **Output:** 
    *   Verified merchant account active on the platform.
    *   Products published under the unified catalog with side-by-side comparative pricing visible to shoppers.
*   **Quality Check:**
    *   Ensure photos and product specifications are accurate and clear.
    *   Verify prices fall within reasonable market ranges (no placeholder prices like 0 IQD or 999,999 IQD).
    *   Ensure duplicate listings are grouped properly rather than published as separate spam pages.

---

### Process 2: Order Processing & Delivery Fulfillment

*   **Input:** 
    *   Customer order placed via the app/website (selected product, delivery address, chosen payment method: Cash on Delivery, FastPay, or Qi Card).
*   **Steps:**
    1.  **Order Notification:** System dispatches an instant alert to the selected merchant’s dashboard/phone.
    2.  **Merchant Confirmation & Packaging:** The seller confirms stock availability and securely packs the product.
    3.  **Courier Dispatch:** Platform automatically generates a tracking waybill and books a pickup with the local courier partner.
    4.  **Pickup & Transit:** Courier collects the package from the local store and transports it to the buyer’s address.
    5.  **Delivery & Payment Collection:** Courier hands over the item, collects payment (if COD), and confirms drop-off via the delivery app.
    6.  **Settlement:** Platform deducts the standard tiered fee and settles the remaining balance to the merchant.
*   **Output:** 
    *   Successfully delivered product to the customer.
    *   Payment collected and merchant payout queued.
    *   Order marked completed in the system.
*   **Quality Check:**
    *   **Post-Delivery Automated Ping:** Instant automated 1-click WhatsApp/SMS check to the customer: *"Did you receive the correct item in good condition at the agreed price?"*
    *   **Delivery SLA Monitoring:** Track courier pickup and transit times to ensure orders arrive within the agreed local window.
    *   **Merchant Rating Update:** Log delivery success or failure to adjust the merchant’s platform reliability score.
