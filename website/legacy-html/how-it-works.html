How Cheapify Works

We eliminated the biggest headaches of online shopping in Iraq: hidden prices, endless DM delays, duplicate listings, and unpredictable delivery.

01

Search once for any item.

On other marketplaces, if five merchants sell the same Anker charger, you see five separate listings with different titles, photos, and messy descriptions. On Cheapify, every identical product is cataloged under one single master page. You see the product once, with full technical specs and clear photos.

Single-SKU Catalog Zero Duplicates Verified Tech Specs
02

Compare every verified store in real time.

Inside the product page, every verified merchant who has stock is displayed. We rank them transparently by Total Doorstep Cost (Item Price + Delivery Fee) and delivery speed. No hidden charges, no "price in DM", and no artificial sponsor bias.

Best Value Tag Item + Delivery = Doorstep Price Real-Time Stock
03

Checkout in 30 seconds with Cash on Delivery.

Pick the best store for your budget and timeline. Skip long registration forms: just enter your phone number, drop your location pin, and confirm. Our delivery partner collects the sealed package from the store and brings it to your doorstep. You pay only after receiving and inspecting your package.

Buyer Guarantees

Built on total buyer protection.

Cheapify is engineered to make online shopping as safe and reliable as walking into your favorite local store.

48-Hour Return Window

If an item arrives faulty, damaged, or wrong, you have a full 48 hours to request a return. The merchant pays for return delivery.

Tamper-Evident Delivery

Every order is dispatched in an opaque, security-taped mailer with a unique waybill number. Couriers do not open packages.

Zero Upfront Risk

You never pay before holding the item. Cash on Delivery is available on 100% of orders, with electronic payment (FIB, Qi, FastPay) arriving soon.
