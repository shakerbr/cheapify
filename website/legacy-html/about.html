The problem with social commerce

In the Kurdistan Region and Iraq, e-commerce has grown rapidly, but shopping online is still messy and fragmented. The vast majority of transactions happen across hundreds of disconnected Instagram pages. The exact same charger, perfume, or kitchen gadget is frequently listed for 25,000 IQD on one page and 45,000 IQD on another—just streets away.

To find the real price, a shopper has to take screenshots, send five or ten direct messages asking "سعرها كم ؟", and wait hours for store admins to reply. Even then, duplicate listings on existing apps show the same product over and over without letting you compare who has the best deal.

"Online shopping shouldn't require sending twenty direct messages just to find out who has stock and what it costs. Cheapify brings transparent pricing to local retail."

Our solution: Single-SKU Comparison

We built Cheapify around a simple, uncompromising principle: One master page per product.

When you look up an item on Cheapify, you don't see chaotic duplicates. You see that single product with every verified local store offering it, ranked clearly by total doorstep price (item price + courier fee) and delivery speed. No hidden fees, no DM friction, and no price manipulation.

Empowering local retail merchants

Cheapify is not built to replace local shop owners; it is designed to empower them. Traditional e-commerce platforms charge aggressive fees of 12% to 25% or demand exclusive inventory. On Cheapify, local merchants keep 96% of every sale, pay 0% commission in their first month, keep their Instagram presence, and receive doorstep courier pickups directly from their shop counter.

Our launch and expansion roadmap

Cheapify launches with an initial founding cohort of verified local stores, establishing a strong, reliable operational base before expanding across Erbil, Mosul, Sulaymaniyah, and all of Iraq.
