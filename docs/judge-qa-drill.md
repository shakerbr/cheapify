## A. Opening and problem

1. Are the two Anker listings really the same product? — Same Anker 25,000 mAh / 165 W on the same Miswag search screen, 100,000 vs 200,000 IQD.
2. Maybe the 200,000 listing is old or a mistake? — Live on 20 Oct 2026 beside the 100,000 one; that is the shopper's problem, you cannot tell which is real.
3. How was the survey done and is it representative? — 199 responses (179 shoppers, 20 merchants), Arabic/Kurdish/English, self-selected, 168 Arabic and 10 Kurdish; strong directional evidence, the $100 pilot tests real buying.
4. Do you have more examples? — Six documented product cases, Appendix A5.

## B. Solution and product

5. How do you know two listings are the same product? — Barcode first; otherwise name, specs, photos, and a reviewer approves.
6. Can a store pay to rank first? — No. Lowest doorstep total, then delivery speed, then rating. Never for sale.
7. Who handles the cash? — Never us; courier collects COD and transfers electronically; merchants paid 48 h after delivery.
8. Why would merchants with higher prices join? — Honest local stores want volume without ads; nearby delivery wins on doorstep total; nobody is cheapest on everything; free to list.
9. Why would a merchant pay a fee when Instagram is free? — Instagram costs hours of "price?" DMs; we send ready orders and charge ~3.8% only on delivered orders.

## C. Market, customers, competition

10. Where do 890,000 and $30 million come from? — Duhok-area census 1.06M x Iraq's 83.8% internet rate and $1.32B e-commerce market; population-share estimates, likely conservative (80% urban).
11. How many shoppers do you need? — Break-even 585 orders/month = ~390 regular shoppers out of ~890,000.
12. Why can't Miswag just copy you? — Their catalog is one listing per seller; single-SKU forces their own sellers into direct price competition.
13. How many merchants are there in Duhok? — ~8 valid leads from the survey; first 20 founding stores signed in person.

## D. Business model and finance

14. What is the 335 IQD? — Per order: WhatsApp 90 + OTP 25 + returns 20 + failed-delivery re-attempts 200.
15. Is the 500 IQD delivery margin real, and what if the contract falls through? — Fast Delivery quote: 3,000 less 500 = 2,500. Without it profit is 615 IQD/order and break-even ~1,060 orders/month; slower, still positive.
16. Isn't break-even just when you pay the grant back? — Two milestones: monthly break-even month 4; payback month 8.
17. What if orders are half of plan? — Low point -$2,750 in month 6, profitable every month from month 7, recovery ~20 months; no out-of-pocket cost for you because runway is funded.
18. What if the courier delays sending the cash? — Merchants are paid only after the 48 h window AND the courier transfer arrives; we never pay out cash we do not hold.
19. Which exchange rate? — 1,500 IQD/USD, Central Bank of Iraq official rate, 7 Oct 2026.

## E. Funding (expect the most pressure here)

20. Why exactly $5,480? — Six real, computed lines: pilot $100, registration $1,000, setup $710, founding-store offer $130, runway $1,340, growth $2,200. No reserve, no salary.
21. What if we award less? — Fallback tiers $3,280 (drops the growth push) and $2,710 (minimum self-funding launch).
22. NEW — With five awards, what is the smallest amount you could still launch on? — $2,710 is the floor today. If a 4th/5th award is below that, say which line you would cut first (growth push, then runway, keeping pilot + registration) and that you would still run the $100 pilot. Prepare this answer; amounts are unannounced, so avoid promising a specific plan.
23. What happens to unused runway? — Released only against actual losses; ~$575 unused on plan, reported back.
24. How do you report progress? — Monthly: delivered orders, gross sales, active stores, repeat-order rate, average delivery time.
25. Why run a pilot before registering? — 15-20 hand-run orders for $100 prove order size and delivery before committing $1,000.

## F. Likely judge questions not in the guide (prepare short answers)

26. Why you, and can one person do this? — Computer engineer; software and UX video built in-house at $0 cash cost; gap is in sales/ops, covered by courier partner and founding stores.
27. What do you do in the first 30 days after winning? — Run the $100 pilot, collect lawyer/accountant quotes, start registration, sign first founding stores.
28. What is your biggest risk? — Traction (merchants and shoppers both needed); mitigated by founding offer, ~8 leads, 97% of shoppers noticing price gaps and 88% willing to use a comparison app.
29. What happens if you win nothing? — Say plainly what you will do (e.g. run the $100 pilot self-funded and continue). Decide this answer before Nov 5.
30. Why does this matter beyond Duhok? — Phased rollout: Duhok metro, then Erbil/Mosul/Sulaymaniyah, then all Iraq.
