### 2.2 When the Money "Comes Back"

The grant is not repaid — but the venture stops needing it and then earns back everything it consumed:

- **Monthly break-even — M4** (~585 orders/mo ≈ 20/day ≈ 390 regular shoppers): revenue covers monthly costs; losses stop growing.
- **Cumulative recovery — ~M8** (~1,850 orders/mo): every dinar of the $2,180 low point earned back. On the fast ramp (driven by the $2,200 growth push), recovery lands in M6 and the entire $5,480 grant is earned back in cumulative profits by M7–M8 (or by M10 on the planned curve).
- Beyond M8 the venture funds its own expansion from profit (+1.4M IQD/mo at ~2,000 orders).

## 5. De-Risking Plan

1. **Validation already underway** — n=199 market survey (Oct 2026) and a verified courier quote completed *before* asking for money; waitlist of ~52 shoppers and ~8 merchants exists today.
2. **$100 buys certainty first** — the concierge pilot runs before registration and any growth spend. If the core assumptions fail, the remaining funds aren't spent.
3. **Runway released against actual losses** — capped at the slow case; undrawn balance reported back.
4. **Bounded incentives, anti-fraud by design** — every reward gated on delivered orders, capped monthly, non-withdrawable credits (`platform/referral_and_growth_incentives.md`).
5. **Three scenarios modeled** — the venture survives even at half the planned volume (slow case).
6. **No founder salary** — the ask can't be consumed as personal income.
