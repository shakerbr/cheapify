## 2. The Core Problem
* **Primary Catalyst — Wild Price Variation:** Identical branded items sell with arbitrary 5,000 to 45,000+ IQD markups across different shops, Instagram pages, and app listings with zero transparency.
* **The "Interrogation Tax":** Online transactions are trapped inside Instagram/Facebook/TikTok DMs because prices are hidden ("DM for price").
* **Information Vacuum:** Posts lack prices, variant options (colors/sizes), exact measures, and live stock status.
* **Repetitive Chat Friction:** Forces buyers into an exhausting 6-message interrogation ("Price?", "Color?", "Size?", "Delivery?").
* **Duplicate Clutter:** Multi-seller platforms show dozens of duplicate listings for the same item rather than grouping sellers.
* **Seller Data Chaos:** Merchants manage stock and orders manually in notebooks or chat threads with zero price intelligence.

---

## 3. The Solution Architecture
* **Single-SKU Master Catalog:** Exactly one master product page per unique barcode/SKU with clear variant selectors (color, size, measurements) and live stock status.
* **Best-Value Ranking Engine:** Side-by-side merchant comparison table with an algorithmically awarded "Best Value" badge based strictly on:
  - **Priority 0 (Most Important):** Price
  - **Priority 1:** Delivery Time
  - **Priority 2:** Rating and Reviews
* **Frictionless Checkout:** 1-click checkout supporting Cash on Delivery (COD) and local e-wallets (FastPay, FIB, Qi Card).
* **Automated Dispatch:** Instant WhatsApp Cloud API order routing to merchants and local 3PL couriers (e.g. ZMC Express).
* **Price Drop Intelligence:** Automated notifications to merchants when competitors lower prices on shared SKUs.
