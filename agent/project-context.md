---

## 2. The Core Problem
* **The "Interrogation Tax":** Online shopping in Iraq is trapped inside Instagram/Facebook/TikTok DMs.
* **Information Vacuum:** Posts lack prices ("DM for price"), variant choices (colors/sizes), precise measures, live stock status, and clear delivery terms.
* **Friction:** Forces buyers into an exhausting 6-message interrogation ("Price?", "Color?", "Size?", "Delivery?").
* **Arbitrary Disparity:** Identical items sell with 5,000 to 45,000 IQD markups across different pages.
* **Duplicate Clutter:** Multi-seller apps show dozens of identical duplicate listings instead of grouped offers.

---

## 3. The Solution Architecture
* **Single-SKU Master Catalog:** Exactly one master product page per unique barcode/SKU with clear variant selectors (color, size, measurements) and live stock status.
* **Best-Value Ranking Engine:** Side-by-side merchant comparison table with an algorithmically awarded Jade Green (`#148C53`) "Best Value" badge.
* **Frictionless Checkout:** 1-click checkout supporting Cash on Delivery (COD) and local e-wallets (FastPay, FIB, Qi Card).
* **Automated Dispatch:** Instant WhatsApp Cloud API order routing to merchants and local 3PL couriers (e.g. ZMC Express).
* **Price Drop Intelligence:** Automated notifications to merchants when competitors lower prices on shared SKUs.

---

## 4. The 4 Program Pillars
1. **Entrepreneurship (30h):** Problem-solution fit, TAM/SAM/SOM, dual personas, validation survey.
2. **Marketing (30h):** Two-sided GTM funnels (shopper & merchant), brand identity, 30-day content calendar.
3. **Finance Management (30h):** $2,200 GIZ seed grant ask, 88.6% gross margin, $0 cash dev fees, break-even at 6 orders/day.
4. **Business Modeling (30h):** Business Model Canvas, 3 core SOPs (Onboarding, 3PL Order Flow, Returns).
